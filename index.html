About the Conference

Where global research meets
the heritage of Bengal

The conference aims to create a vibrant platform for scientists, researchers, academicians, and industry experts to share knowledge, exchange ideas, and explore innovative solutions. By fostering interdisciplinary synergy among science, technology, health science, and innovation, it advances sustainable development, environmental resilience, and human well-being.

Objectives
To disseminate original research, foster international collaboration, and connect emerging scholars with world-class mentors across Computational Science and Artificial Intelligence, Biotechnology, Biodiversity, Environment, Health Science, and Sustainable Technological Innovation.
Vision & Scope
A premier annual forum in Eastern India covering theory, applications and systems - from foundational algorithms to deployed, real-world platforms.
Why Participate
Keynotes by global leaders, indexed proceedings, best-paper awards, doctoral consortium, industry exhibits and networking across 25+ countries.
Who Can Participate?
  • Faculties / Scientists
  • Corporate Delegates
  • Research Scholars / Students
  • Foreign Delegates
Concept Note

Scientific progress increasingly depends on interdisciplinary integration. This conference highlights the synergy among Computational Science & Artificial Intelligence, Sustainable Technological Innovation, Biotechnology, Environment, Biodiversity, and Health Science to foster transformative research and sustainable solutions. By promoting cross-disciplinary collaboration, it seeks to bridge fundamental science and technological innovation for addressing complex societal and environmental challenges while advancing sustainable development and human well-being.
