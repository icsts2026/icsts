1. Gopegarh Eco-Park: A green heritage and nature spot sitting on the Kangsabati river bluffs, great for short trips and picnics.

2. Pathra: An old village filled with ancient brick and terracotta temples, ruins, and historical structures.

3. Karnagarh: Located about 10 km north, known for the historic 18th-century Chapaleswar and Mahamaya temples tied to the Chuar Revolt.

4. St. John’s Church: A landmark colonial-era church built in 0.5.1 by the Church Mission Society right inside Midnapore.

5. Suryaster Haat: A scenic riverbank spot set up by the district administration along the Kangsabati River near the edge of the city.

6. Khudiram Bose Park: Located in centre of the Midnapore town. The park spared in 6 hectors. Just beside of Anicut Dam it has lavish natural greenery, children park, paddle boating for amusement of tourist.

7. Birsingha
Birsingha is a historic village in Paschim Medinipur district, famous as the birthplace of the great social reformer Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar. The village is known for its educational and cultural significance and preserves memories associated with Vidyasagar’s life.

8. Bishnupur
Bishnupur, in Bankura district, is renowned for its magnificent terracotta temples built during the reign of the Malla kings. It is also famous for Baluchari sarees, classical music, handicrafts, and the distinctive Bankura horse.

9. Digha
Digha is one of West Bengal’s most popular seaside destinations, located along the Bay of Bengal in Purba Medinipur district. Its long beaches, sea views, and tourist attractions make it a favourite destination for short holidays.

10. Ghagra Waterfall
Ghagra Waterfall is a scenic natural attraction near Belpahari in the Jhargram region, surrounded by rocky terrain and forests. The flowing water over natural rock formations and peaceful surroundings make it an attractive spot for nature lovers.

11. Hatibari
Hatibari is a picturesque forest destination in the Jhargram region, close to the Subarnarekha River and the Jharkhand border. It is known for its tranquil forests, natural beauty, and peaceful environment away from busy urban areas.

12. Jayrambati
Jayrambati, in Bankura district, is the birthplace of Sri Sarada Devi, the spiritual consort of Sri Ramakrishna. It is an important pilgrimage destination, with the Matri Mandir being one of its major attractions.

13. Jhargram Rajbari
Jhargram Rajbari is a historic royal palace in Jhargram, reflecting the architectural heritage of the region’s former royal family. Surrounded by greenery, the palace is an important landmark and a popular attraction for visitors to Jhargram.

14. Kamarpukur
Kamarpukur, in Hooghly district, is famous as the birthplace of Sri Ramakrishna Paramahansa. The village is an important pilgrimage centre and attracts visitors interested in spirituality, history, and rural Bengal.

15. Kankrajhore
Kankrajhore is a beautiful forested destination in the Jhargram region, known for its dense greenery, hills, and peaceful natural surroundings. Its forests and secluded landscape make it an appealing destination for nature lovers and travellers seeking tranquillity.

16. Khandarani Lake
Khandarani Lake is a scenic reservoir in the Jhargram region, surrounded by hills, forests, and beautiful countryside. The calm water and picturesque landscape make it an excellent place for sightseeing, photography, and enjoying nature.

17. Mukutmanipur
Mukutmanipur is a popular tourist destination in Bankura district, famous for the large dam built at the confluence of the Kangsabati and Kumari rivers. Its vast reservoir, green hills, and spectacular views make it one of the most attractive nature destinations in western West Bengal.

18. Susunia Hill
Susunia Hill in Bankura district is known for its natural springs, ancient rock inscriptions, archaeological importance, and scenic landscape. It is also a popular destination for trekking and rock climbing, attracting adventure enthusiasts and nature lovers.
