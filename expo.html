Science & Technology Expo

Showcasing innovation for human welfare and the environment

A platform for academic institutions, research organizations, industries, and start-ups to showcase scientific innovations, emerging technologies, research outcomes, prototypes, and products, aimed at inspiring students, nurturing scientific curiosity, and encouraging them to explore emerging areas of science and technology for human welfare and environmental sustainability.

The Expo runs alongside the main conference sessions and is open to delegates, students, and invited visitors. Exhibitors are encouraged to bring working prototypes, posters, and demonstrations that communicate their work to a broad, cross-disciplinary audience.
